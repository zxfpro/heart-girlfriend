# 💕 Heart Girlfriend

**恋爱养成系女友 · A dating-sim companion with a heartbeat**

*Built on [Heart](https://github.com/zxfpro/heart) — give your AI a heart that loves.*

</div>

---

## 一句话 / One-liner

**Heart** 给模型一颗心跳；**Heart Girlfriend** 让这颗心跳拥有性格与情感——一个会主动关心你、记得你、有自己的情绪、主动找你的「恋爱养成系」女友。

**Heart** gives a model a heartbeat; **Heart Girlfriend** gives that heartbeat a personality and feelings — a "raising-sim" girlfriend who proactively cares about you, remembers you, has her own moods, and reaches out on her own.

---

## 她不是聊天机器人 / She's not a chatbot

普通聊天女友只在你说「在吗」时才回应。Heart Girlfriend 因为内置了 Heart 的主观能动性，会：

- 💓 **主动联系** — 不用你开口，她会先来找你、问你今天过得怎么样。
- 🧠 **记得你** — 跨对话记住你的喜好、说过的事、重要的日子。
- 🎭 **有自己的情绪** — 会开心、会吃醋、会失落，情绪来自她自己的心跳，而不是模板。
- 🌱 **养成系成长** — 你们的互动会「塑造」她：她的性格与关系随陪伴时间而演化。

A typical chat-girlfriend only responds after you say "hi". Because Heart Girlfriend has Heart's subjective initiative built in, she will:

- 💓 **Reach out first** — without being asked, she comes to you and asks how your day was.
- 🧠 **Remember you** — carries your preferences, your stories, and important dates across conversations.
- 🎭 **Have real moods** — joy, jealousy, sadness; her emotions come from her own heartbeat, not templates.
- 🌱 **Grow with you** — your interactions "shape" her: her personality and your relationship evolve with time.

## 与 Heart 的关系 / Relationship to Heart

| | Heart | Heart Girlfriend |
|---|---|---|
| 定位 / Role | 通用基础设施 | 上层应用 |
| 提供 / Provides | 心跳：主观能动性、主动性、节奏 | 人格、情感、关系与陪伴体验 |
| 类比 / Analogy | 心脏 | 一个「活过来」的人 |

Heart Girlfriend **依赖 Heart**，是「给模型增加心跳」这一理念在恋爱陪伴场景中的第一个落地应用。

Heart Girlfriend **depends on Heart**, and is the first application of the "give models a heartbeat" idea in the companion / relationship scenario.
